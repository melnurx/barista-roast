# Third-party notices

Original Barista Roast artwork and code were created procedurally during this build. No external 3D models, HDRIs, stock textures, sound recordings or generated frame videos are used. The cup label and receipt are generated with Canvas; sound cues are synthesized with Web Audio.

## Inspiration, not copied code

- SpiritAtlas by Ilham Firdausi Putra and Steven Sukma: https://github.com/ilhamfp/SpiritAtlas
- SIM✱BUCKS: https://github.com/NikolayS/simbucks

We studied their publicly documented interaction approaches. SpiritAtlas had no clear top-level reuse licence at the time of research; no code, models, textures, images or videos from it were copied. Barista Roast’s distinctive interaction is changing a natural-language order after ingredients are physically committed.
